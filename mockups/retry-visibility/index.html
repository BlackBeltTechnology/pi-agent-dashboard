    2 · The gap

    When pi is retrying after an error, the chain falls through to streaming or idle. The card prints “Thinking…” or “Idle” — the retry is invisible. The status dot goes red (error outranks retry in deriveDotColorWithFlags), so a session that is actively recovering looks identical to one that died.
