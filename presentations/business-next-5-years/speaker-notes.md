## Anticipated questions

- *"Isn't this just the AI hype cycle again?"* — Point at slide 5 and 22. The forecast names its own failure modes and the jagged frontier is in the data, not the marketing.
- *"Will AI replace sales?"* — Slide 11 and 14: displacement concentrates in clerical and entry-level work; the WEF net figure is +78M jobs. The role changes shape, not existence.
- *"Why should we change pricing?"* — Slide 9 and 18: buyers now gate on measured outcomes. Outcome pricing is a response to buyer behaviour, not a pricing fad.
