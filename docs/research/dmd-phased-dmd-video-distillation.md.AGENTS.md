Method: DMD three nets generator `G_φ` (student trainable), fake score `F_θ` (trainable, tracks student), teacher `T` (frozen real score CFG), `G`+`F` init from teacher; objective minimize reverse KL `KL(p_fake ‖ p_real)`, gradient ≈ `E[w_t(T(x_t,t)−F(x_t,t))dG/dφ]`, no backprop through T/F → scalable vs SiD; original DMD adds regression `λ_reg=0.25` + LPIPS on precomputed teacher (noise, multi-step sample) pairs expensive paired dataset, stabilizes mode coverage; DMD2 removes regression loss, adds two-timescale update (fake score more often e.g. 5 F per G), GAN loss on fake-score features, multi-step backward sim with SGTS (random stop step j, grad only last step); assumptions A1 F converged per G update A2 F unbiased (same param/target as teacher); data-free in principle needs only prompts (+ images I2V), GAN variant needs real/teacher videos (FastVideo 600k synthetic latents `Wan-Syn_77x448x832_600k`; FastGen Wan DMD2 data from Wan2.1 14B VidProM prompts); weakness one-step/SGTS → diversity loss, slower motion, close-up bias, reverse-KL mode-seeking. Phased DMD: splits SNR range into subintervals (phases), each phase one expert `G_k` mapping `p(x_{t_{k-1}})→p(x_{t_k})`, progressive low→high SNR ProGAN-like NOT progressive distillation; backward sim stops at intermediate `t_k` not clean x0, diffuses `x_{t_k}` to `t~T(t_k,1)` (wider range beats `(t_k,t_{k-1})`); fake score per phase re-init from teacher not prior phase, subinterval score-matching objective paper Eq.11/13 flow target `((α_s²σ_t+α_tσ_s²)/(α_s²σ_{t|s}))ε − x_s/α_s`, stable form `clamp(1/σ_{t|s}²,[0,10])·‖σ_{t|s}ψ − ((α_s²σ_t+α_tσ_s²)/α_s²)ε + (σ_{t|s}/α_s)x_s‖²`, naive target `‖ψ−(ε−x_s)‖²` biased breaks A2 key trap; output = few-step MoE one expert per phase even if teacher not MoE aligns Wan2.2 A14B high/low-noise experts; compatible SGTS 4 steps 2 phases (Wan2.2); no GAN no regression data-free.
