Four different radii (8/12/5/10px), two token systems (--severity-* vs raw red-500/amber-500), three log treatments (collapsible <pre> · prose · fixed 3 truncated lines), two dismiss glyphs, two alert icons. No shared "log" primitive.

One .msg (severity accent bar + icon + title + sub + action-pills + mdiClose) drives spawn, session, and missing-tool. One .logblock (labelled header · copy · collapse/expand · mono inset) drives stderr AND flow logs. All radii, severity tokens, icons unified.
