Redline mode (default): your text with issues dotted-underlined by kind and the fix shown inline as a green ghost — nothing hides behind hover. Compact mode: a denser wavy squiggle; hover/focus a word for a popover with Apply / Ignore. Original / Corrected: read-only before/after previews. Kind colors: spelling·red, grammar·blue, punctuation·orange, style·purple. Per-change apply AND Apply all in every mode. The chosen mode is remembered and restored next time (reload this page to see it stick).
