This is enforced centrally by `runtime/scene.ts` `sortAboveVeil`, applied to every attached effect — effects do NOT set renderOrder themselves. Promotion re-ranks `backgroundForTopic` (cheapest-wins, ties by id): `geo`→`geo-fragments`, `agents`→`agent-depth`, `trust`→`trust-ledger`, `compute`→`fab-wafer`, `money`→`capital-flows`. See change: deck3d-cinematic-worlds. `video-screen` plays a clip on a framed screen in the scene: panel is UNLIT `MeshBasicMaterial` (a recording must keep its own pixels, not be tinted by the key light), bezel is lit metal and casts shadow, geometry re-fits to the clip aspect on first frame. Clip MUST be same-origin — a `file://` source taints the canvas and `texImage2D` refuses it; use `deck3d serve`'s asset route or `render --embed-video`. Playback is gated by `FxHandle.setActive`, driven by `runtime/index.ts` `settle()`, so a clip runs only while its slide is settled and a pinned time renders a stable frame. See change: deck3d-cinematic-worlds. `tokens`/`rings`/`swarm`/`particles` delegate to `runtime/backgrounds.ts`; the rest are permissive three.js-example ports behind the interface. |
